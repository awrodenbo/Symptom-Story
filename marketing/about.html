About

Health history without the clinical dashboard.

Symptom Story exists to make personal health history easier to record when attention, time, and energy are limited.

Why we are building it

Experiences can be difficult to remember and harder to summarize during a short appointment. Symptom Story creates a quiet place to record what happened, in your own words, without turning those entries into a diagnosis.

Our product principles

Compassion without pressure

No streak anxiety, guilt-based reminders, or forced positivity.

Observation without interpretation

We summarize what was entered without claiming cause, improvement, or treatment effect.

Privacy without ambiguity

Clear boundaries, user-scoped records, and no advertising business model.

Access from the start

Accessibility is a product requirement, not a later polish pass.

Medical boundary

Symptom Story is not a medical device, crisis service, clinician, or substitute for professional care. Product and safety decisions require ongoing review with qualified clinical, privacy, security, accessibility, and legal professionals.
