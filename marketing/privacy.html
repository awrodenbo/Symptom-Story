Privacy

Private by design, clear by default.

Your health history should never become the price of using a helpful tool.

The short version: We do not include advertising trackers or sell health data. Journal entries are never sent to AI. Optional appointment drafts send only the disclosed check-ins, medication list, and questions after you consent.

Your account, your records

Records are associated with an authenticated account. Database row-level security is designed to restrict reads and writes to that account. Defense-in-depth filters are also used in application requests.

What the app stores

When you choose to enter them, Symptom Story stores profile preferences, check-ins, medication records, medication logs, and journal reflections. It does not need a contact list, precise location, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier for its core experience.

Control and portability

You can export the records available to your account and request account deletion from within the app. Information sent through the operating system share sheet is governed by the destination you select.

Security boundaries

Native sessions use operating-system protected storage. Supabase authentication and row-level security provide the identity and authorization layer. No system eliminates all risk; review the project security policy for safeguards and known limitations.
