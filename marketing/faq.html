FAQ

Questions, answered gently.

Straightforward answers about what Symptom Story does, what it does not do, and how your records are handled.

Who is Symptom Story for?

It is designed for people who have been diagnosed by a licensed healthcare professional with PMDD or postpartum depression and want to maintain a personal health history.

Does the app diagnose or screen for a condition?

No. It does not diagnose, screen, predict recovery, recommend treatment, or replace professional medical care.

How long does a check-in take?

The focused six-step flow is designed to take under two minutes. Questions other than the minimum needed to save are optional.

Does Symptom Story use AI to interpret my journal?

Journal entries are never sent to AI. If you explicitly choose an appointment draft, the disclosed check-ins, medication list, and questions are sent to the configured AI provider so you can review and edit the result.

Can anyone else see my records?

Database policies are designed so an authenticated account can access only its own records. No internet-connected service is risk-free, and independent security validation remains necessary before production use.

What happens if I record an immediate safety concern?

The app displays a static, calm prompt to seek immediate human support. It does not generate a crisis response or lock you into the screen.

Can I export or delete my information?

Yes. The app includes export and account deletion controls. Backup retention and deletion are governed by the eventual production operations policy.
