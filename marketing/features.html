Features

Support for the story only you can tell.

Small daily tools that help you record experiences, understand your history, and prepare for care conversations.

♥

Two-minute check-ins

Move through mood, sleep, energy, symptoms, medication, and an optional reflection one card at a time.

◷

Medication context

Keep a personal record of medication names, schedules, and when you marked a dose as taken.

↗

Personal trends

Review charts made from your own entries, paired with clear text alternatives and careful boundaries.

□

Private journal

Capture a short reflection without prompts that pressure you to write more or stay positive.

⇧

Your export

Export self-reported records when you choose. The app does not decide who should receive them.

♢

Support and Safety

Reach a calm, static path toward immediate human support from every primary screen.

Built around the day you are actually having.

Every question is optional except the minimum needed to save an entry.
