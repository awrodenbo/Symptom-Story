# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community for contributors of every identity, background, body, ability, health history, and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Treat lived experience as valuable without asking anyone to disclose private health information.
- Give actionable feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Respect names, pronouns, boundaries, and accessibility needs.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and make space for others.
- Keep project spaces free of diagnosis, unsolicited treatment advice, and crisis counseling.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing, deliberate misgendering, publishing private information, sharing health data without consent, or retaliating against a report is not acceptable. Do not use project channels to promote products, recruit vulnerable people, or provide unlicensed medical advice.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include unnecessary health information. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when behavior threatens community safety. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with conflicts of interest disclosed and reassigned.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted for a privacy-conscious health technology community.
